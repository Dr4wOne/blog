Ten directions for blog.dr4w.io

Every prototype is a working build of the real home page and the real article, with all figures and widgets, in light and dark. Scores come from outside judges: Lighthouse 13.4 (mobile, simulated throttling), axe-core (WCAG 2.2 AA + best practice, light and dark, 390 and 1440 px), and the taste-skill gate (inspect.mjs, commit f624f37) with only the allowances in ADR 0006. Judged 2026-10-15.

Essay home article

One serif column at about 68 characters, the way long essays are read on paper.
Deep dives float into the right margin on wide screens (Tufte-style sidenotes) and sit inline on phones.
Nothing competes with the text: no cards, one quiet blue for links.

Typefaces
Source Serif 4 (text and display), JetBrains Mono (code only)
Brand link to dr4w.io
Dark mode uses the landing page's #0A0A0F ground; same dr4w wordmark and author link.
taste-skill
design-taste-frontend, minimalist-ui, full-output-enforcement; dials: variance 3, motion 2, density 3
Weakest point
The quietest home page of the ten; with only one issue it relies on the writing to hold attention.

Swiss grid home article

A strict 12-column grid with one grotesk; hierarchy comes from scale and position, not decoration.
Huge tight headlines, a sticky contents rail on the left, thick black rules as section markers.
Ends on a full-width wordmark, like a poster.

Typefaces
Schibsted Grotesk (everything), JetBrains Mono (code only)
Brand link to dr4w.io
Black/white with one red; dark mode on #0A0A0F; the oversized dr4w wordmark echoes the landing hero name.
taste-skill
design-taste-frontend, industrial-brutalist-ui (toned down), full-output-enforcement; dials: variance 5, motion 2, density 3
Weakest point
Huge grotesk headlines make long section titles wrap; titles must stay short.

A cover-sized issue number opens each issue, then a drop cap and a serif column.

Deep dives are boxed sidebars hanging into the right margin.

Typefaces

Brand link to dr4w.io

Index-first home article

The home page is the table of contents: each issue's huge number, title, summary and every section, linked.
Article pages are deliberately quiet: modest title, narrow column, no chrome.
Scales well as issues accumulate.

Typefaces

Brand link to dr4w.io
Big numerals mirror the issue-number URLs; dark mode on #0A0A0F; green accent is its own.
taste-skill
design-taste-frontend, minimalist-ui, full-output-enforcement; dials: variance 6, motion 2, density 3
Weakest point
Article pages are deliberately plain; the design shines only once there are several issues.
